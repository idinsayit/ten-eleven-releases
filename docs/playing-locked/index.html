Ten Eleven: playing on with the phone locked

Ten Eleven plays recorded prayers and meditations. Once a person has pressed play, the piece keeps playing when the phone is locked or another app is in front. This is the one thing the app uses a foreground service for.

What the film shows

  • A prayer, On waking, is opened and started.
  • The phone is locked. The prayer plays on.
  • The locked screen shows the prayer by its name, with a button to pause it.
  • The phone is unlocked. The prayer has kept playing all the while, as the time on its page shows.
  • The prayer is paused from its page.

A meditation is played by the same service, in the same way.

The film was made on an emulated phone running Android 14, with a made-up person signed in. It has no sound.
